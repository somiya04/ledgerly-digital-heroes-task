Questions

Frequently asked

Can I switch plans later?

Yes. You can move between Starter, Growth, and Scale at any time; billing adjusts on your next cycle.

Is there a setup fee?

No. All plans include onboarding and bank feed setup at no extra cost.

What happens if I exceed my invoice limit?

We notify you before you hit the limit and let you upgrade instantly with no service interruption.

Do you offer annual billing?

Yes, annual billing is available on all plans at a reduced effective monthly rate.
