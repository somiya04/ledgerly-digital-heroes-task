Product

Every invoice, tracked from draft to reconciled.

Three connected modules — Invoicing, Approvals, and Reconciliation — that share one ledger instead of three disconnected tools.

Module 01

Invoicing

Create and send invoices from templates that match your brand, with line-item tax rules that adjust per client automatically.

—

Recurring schedules

Set an invoice to repeat weekly, monthly, or on a custom billing cycle.

—

Multi-currency

Bill clients in their local currency; Ledgerly converts for your books automatically.

—

Payment links

Every invoice ships with a hosted pay link — no separate payment processor to configure.

Module 02

Approvals

Route invoices to the right person based on amount, department, or vendor — and escalate automatically if they stall.

—

Threshold rules

Anything over $5,000 routes to a director; under that, a team lead can approve directly.

—

Auto-escalation

An invoice untouched for 48 hours moves up automatically, so nothing sits silently.

—

Mobile approvals

Approve or flag an invoice from a phone in under ten seconds.

Module 03

Reconciliation

Connect a bank feed once. Ledgerly matches payments to open invoices and only surfaces what needs a human decision.

—

Auto-matching

Payments that match an open invoice exactly are reconciled without any manual step.

—

Exception queue

Partial payments and mismatches land in one queue instead of buried in a bank statement.

—

Close-ready exports

Export a reconciled ledger to your accounting system in one click at month-end.
