The problem

Spreadsheets don't scale past the tenth approver.

Most finance teams start in Google Sheets and email. It works — until invoices go missing in someone's inbox, approvals stall over Slack, and month-end close takes a week instead of a day.

01

Lost approvals

Invoices wait in inboxes with no record of who has seen them or why they're stuck.

02

Manual reconciliation

Someone still copies bank statement lines into a spreadsheet by hand every month.

03

No audit trail

When a client disputes a charge, nobody can quickly show who approved what, and when.

The Ledgerly way

One ledger. Every invoice, approval, and reconciliation logged in order.

Ledgerly gives every invoice a single, timestamped record from creation to payment — so nothing depends on someone remembering to check an inbox.

A

Route approvals automatically

Set thresholds once. Invoices route to the right approver and escalate if they sit too long.

B

Reconcile in minutes

Bank feeds match against open invoices automatically, flagging only what needs a human look.

C

Export a clean audit trail

Every action is logged with who, what, and when — exportable for any client or auditor.

See your own invoices in Ledgerly before you commit to anything.
